Common questions
Everything you need to know
Is there a free trial for paid plans? +

No, we don't offer a free trial period. However, you can request a free Pro demo and our team will walk you through all the Pro features with a hands-on session. Click "Request a Pro Demo" above to get started.

What counts as a "scan"? +

One scan = one URL analysed across all viewport sizes (mobile 375px, tablet 768px, desktop 1280px) with Playwright + axe-core. Full WCAG report, screenshots, and AI suggestions are generated per scan.

Can I cancel at any time? +

Absolutely. Cancel anytime from your account page. You keep access until the end of your current billing period — no questions asked.

Do you support self-hosted / on-premise? +

The orchestrator is open-source and can be self-hosted. Reach out for enterprise licensing if you need a managed on-premise deployment with SLA guarantees.

Does data leave my browser? +

The Chrome extension runs axe-core entirely in-browser — no page data is sent anywhere. The orchestrator (when used) processes URLs server-side but does not store page content beyond your session.
