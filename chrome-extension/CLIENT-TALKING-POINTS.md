# SiteScope 360 — Client Talking Points

The accessibility tool that fixes the problems instead of just listing them. Works with any website.

## 1. Business use case

- The law now requires websites to work for people with disabilities — enforced across Europe since June 2025, and a common source of lawsuits in the US.
- Spotting the problems is easy and free. Fixing them is what costs time and money.
- Most tools hand you a list of problems. This one fixes them, checks the fix actually worked, and delivers the change to your website.

## 2. What is the AI in this?

- The AI writes the fix, not just the report. It reads the broken part of the page and produces the corrected version, then explains in plain English what's wrong across the whole page and what to tackle first.
- It catches what checklists miss. Automated checklists find about a third of real problems. The AI finds the rest — image descriptions that exist but say nothing useful, "click here" links that tell you nothing, headings that describe nothing, and problems you can only see by looking at the page.
- It checks its own work. It makes the change, tests the page again, tries a different approach if it didn't work, and only asks a person when it's genuinely stuck.
- You can just talk to it. "Fix everything urgent, but don't change our brand colours" — and it can hand the finished change straight to your developers.
- Runs on your own AI account, or entirely on your own machine.

## 3. What makes this different from other tools

- Works with any website, whatever it's built on. Adobe, WordPress, Drupal, Sitecore, Shopify or something custom — it makes no difference. There is nothing to plug into your systems and no integration project.
- Plug and play. It runs in the browser. Open a page, press scan. No installation into your platform, no developer setup, no waiting on IT.
- Other tools stop at the report. This one delivers the fix — and then proves it worked by testing the page again.
- You get evidence, not promises. Every change is re-tested, so you can show what was fixed rather than claim it.
- Your page content never has to leave your computer.

## 4. Is this feature currently available in Adobe?

- No — Adobe doesn't do this today. Adobe's AI helps tag and describe images in its content library, and helps manage marketing work.
- No Adobe product looks at a live web page, writes the correction, applies it, and proves it worked.
- Because it doesn't depend on Adobe, it also covers every non-Adobe website the business owns — so it sits alongside Adobe rather than competing with it.

## 5. Business benefits

- Cuts the expensive part of the work — the fixing, not the finding.
- Gives legal and compliance teams proof that problems were actually fixed, not just a report saying they were found.
- The corrections reach your real website, so they don't disappear the next time the page loads.
- Easy to approve and cheap to run: it works in the browser, most fixes need no AI at all, and your content can stay on your machine.
- One tool for every website you own, however each one was built.

## Positioning summary

A plug-and-play accessibility tool that works with any website, on any platform. It finds the problems, fixes them, proves the fixes worked, and tells you exactly what it couldn't handle and why.
